DM2 Death Match Decision Maker

Can't decide? Give every option a fighter. Winner stays on. The last one standing is your decision.

  1. Enter your options, or a list of people and the gifts they're fighting over. Every option gets a fighter.
  2. Watch them brawl. Winner stays on, or in Prize Fights, two people fight for each gift.
  3. Share the code. Anyone can replay the same fights and the same result.
